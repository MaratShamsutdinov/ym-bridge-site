Choose your player layout

Two control surfaces

The side panel stays attached to Chrome and keeps Last.fm state, the queue and tools nearby. The mini player is a separate resizable window for playback controls and artwork.

Both read the same live Yandex Music track. Switching a layout changes presentation only; playback, likes, seeking and scrobbling continue through the extension.

Mini player layouts

Use the full player for every control, Progress disc for artwork-led listening, or Ambient for a wide window that blends the cover into its sampled color.

Side panel layouts

The complete panel exposes status and tools. Compact views keep playback close: Progress disc emphasizes the cover, Control deck is dense and direct, and Glass card floats the controls over an ambient backdrop.

Cycle or pin a layout

Press the layout button in the upper-right corner to move through the full view and every compact skin. Progress and controls remain live while the interface changes.

To keep one design, open Global settings and select a fixed value under Mini player compact view or Side panel compact view. Choose Cycle all to keep the round-robin behavior.
